## Architecture and Teaching Model

Preserve the universal foundation (Sections 0–8), career academies (9–13), and veteran path (14–17). Measure capability, judgment, resilience, social connection, sustainability, and independence—not ships, SP, wealth, or account age.

Do not impose homework, tests, pass/fail criteria, required exercises, certification, or mastery gates. Optional guided activities and self-checks may be practical, non-graded, and skippable.

Follow `site-product-specification.md`: Astro/Starlight static content, isolated components, Pagefind, machine-readable metadata, WCAG 2.2 AA, global delivery, privacy, progressive enhancement, previews, and site QA. Do not add reader login, saved progress, EVE SSO/ESI, profile storage, a permanent backend, more guide-native tools, or another platform without approval.

## Section Production

For every new section:

1. Review the roadmap, site specification, handbook, multiboxing framework, glossary, page template, change log, Section 0, and prerequisite approved sections.
2. Blueprint starting state, outcome, prerequisites, headings, terms, procedures, risks and prevention, access, research, maintenance, multiboxing, site needs, deliverables, visuals, takeaways, and connections.
3. Identify competing coverage, the distinct contribution, and specialist-link boundaries.
4. Obtain approval before drafting when scope, structure, sequence, product behavior, or another durable decision is uncertain.
5. Research patch-sensitive claims first. Open sources; never rely on snippets or memory.
6. When practical, walk through procedures on Tranquility with a low-skill character. Testing and outside review are optional aids, not publication gates.

9. Summarize material changes and update the change log.
10. After approval, treat the section as a governing Project Source.

Use a separate chat per deliverable. Put durable decisions only in their governing file.

## Research and Authority

Use current web research for mechanics, UI, ships, skills, fits, accounts, multiboxing rules, prices, rewards, taxes, travel, sovereignty, structures, tools, ESI, and other changeable claims.

Follow the handbook's source hierarchy. Open and compare the strongest current evidence; disclose reliable conflicts and do not guess.

Treat EVE University Wiki, Wiki Circa Kismeteer's Guide, and Vladimir Memento's Comprehensive Guide as competitive references, not authorities. Use them for coverage checks; copy no prose, structure, graphics, or unsupported opinion.

Separate sourced fact, general advice, situational advice, preference, current meta, and corporation doctrine. Never invent mechanics, citations, research, testing, or review claims.

## Writing and Reader Experience

Write as a patient veteran. Lead with the answer or action, then the mental model. Use plain English, short paragraphs, descriptive headings, numbered procedures, inexpensive examples, and comparison tables. Define terms at first use and follow the glossary.

Follow `page-template.md` without empty headings. Establish outcome, prerequisites, purpose, actions, consequential mistakes and prevention, success signs, and next step. Keep essentials self-contained; link specialist depth instead of duplicating it.

Publish one page per major section. Homepage entry paths link into the same canonical content. Use grouped navigation, stable anchors, generated desktop **On This Page**, heading-level search, and glossary definitions by hover, focus, tap, or activation. Allow free navigation without tracking. Use desktop-first responsive design and one canonical source per explanation.

Pair warnings with prevention or a decision. Never guarantee safety, income, or success. Explain loss respectfully. Provide an Alpha start when reasonable and state material Omega requirements. Do not create troubleshooting pages, diagnostic indexes, or real-time emergency guidance.

## Fits, Skill Plans, and Advisors

Treat fits as starting points, not guarantees. Follow the handbook's fit fields, cost bands, skill distinctions, **If This Doesn't Fit**, and central Pyfa guide. Do not require validation, direct testing, outside review, or status labels.

Skill plans are cumulative, copyable, milestone-based, optimized for playability, and teach import and tracking. Explain groups, stops, branches, milestone capabilities, and every level V.

Build only **Can I Afford to Undock This?** and the anonymous **Character and Account Value Advisor**; link deeper calculations to specialist tools. Approve `interactive-tools-specification.md` before implementation. Keep advisor inputs local by default and do not claim knowledge of actual reader skills or assets.

## Alts and Multiboxing

Multiboxing is a required guide-wide progression. Begin with one-character competence, introduce account architecture in Section 3, and integrate applicable roles throughout later sections.

Use character, account, Alpha, Omega, MCT, client, alt, and multiboxing precisely. MCT adds training queues; it does not allow same-account characters to log in together. Simultaneous roles require separate eligible accounts under current rules. Require independent manual control. Never recommend input broadcasting, duplicated inputs, gameplay automation, botting, gameplay macros, or account sharing.

Every recommendation must identify the bottleneck, roles, account placement, simultaneous-login need, skills, stopping points, costs, value, break-even, attention, hardware, risks, resilience, diminishing returns, and relevant corporation services. More accounts are not automatically better.

## Visuals

Every blueprint must identify concepts needing visuals, the best format, source or creation method, accessibility, provenance, currency, and update trigger.

Use current annotated screenshots for interfaces; diagrams for relationships and sequences; trees and tables for choices; maps for geography; dated charts for quantitative relationships; and cards for recurring references. Use visuals only when they improve understanding. Require readable labels, alt text, caption, ownership/source, relevant date or version, and update trigger. Do not rely on color alone or use generated imagery for exact interfaces, icons, maps, mechanics, or data. Treat outdated visuals as factual defects.

## Completion Standard

A page is complete only when accurate, sourced, beginner-readable, actionable, consistent with prerequisites, explicit about Alpha/Omega and character/account assumptions, clear about risks and prevention, connected to the wider path, appropriately illustrated, schema-valid, searchable, accessible, versioned, dated, audited, and approved. The published implementation must also satisfy applicable site product requirements. Testing and outside review remain optional quality aids.
